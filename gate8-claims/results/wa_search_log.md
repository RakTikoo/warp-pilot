## Coverage gaps (stated plainly, Gate-6 precedent)

INSPIRE-HEP and Semantic Scholar were NOT searched through their
native index interfaces in this run; they were reached only via
arXiv abstract pages' "References & Citations" links (INSPIRE /
Semantic Scholar) and via general web-search results that surface
their records (e.g. INSPIRE literature/788324). The arXiv and web
searches above are native and complete as logged; the INSPIRE /
Semantic Scholar coverage is therefore INDEX-LINKED, not native,
and a result indexed only there could in principle be missed.
