## Result of the search

No source found — in any query — that (a) closes a constructed
braneworld-throat bulk lift with a Z2 mirror brane (or any
regular completion) and (b) measures the attained cross-throat
geodesic gain of the completed bulk, or (c) prices that
completion by its mirror brane's Israel data, or (d) splits the
unattained 0.480-class infimum into "singularity load-bearing
for size, not for existence". The ingredients are all standard
and published separately: bulk shortcuts (Chung–Freese and
successors), Z2 two-brane RS geometry, Israel junction pricing,
regular braneworld completions (for black holes / with bulk
fluids). The composed statement was not found in print.
