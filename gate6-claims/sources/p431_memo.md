# P43-1 MEMO — Bulk shortcut geodesics in the programme's
# braneworld homes (Worker W1, Phase 43)

**Plain language first.** W3 closed bulk shortcuts for a flat
or cosmologically bent brane: gain exactly zero, or negligibly
small. This item asked the one question W3 did not: in the
programme's own exhibited braneworld *throat* — where an actual
5D bulk now exists on record (P29-3) — can a bulk ray between
two brane points beat light that stays on the brane? Answer:
**yes, as a geodesic fact, and by a large fraction — but only
for pairs on opposite sides of the throat, only because the
bulk pinches shut at the throat, and it still is not
transport.** The bulk radial scale at the throat collapses
toward the bulk singularity that sits 0.47 throat-radii off
the brane, so a ray that dives toward (never reaching) that
singularity crosses the throat region far more cheaply than
brane light crossing on the brane. For the headline pair the
bulk arrival is about half the brane light time. The catch
list is the verdict: the throat and its bulk must simply
*pre-exist* (no formation route exists on record), the
optimal path is not attained inside the bulk (the infimum
lives at the singular boundary; every quoted gain is at a
stated depth cap), and the only carrier that can take the
path is a graviton — no photon, no message-bearing SM
signal, no payload. Verdict: **SHORTCUT REAL, EFFECTIVE
ONLY.** The finite-mass Option-2 row separately caps at
**PARTIAL — BULK UNSHOWN** (its bulk lift was never
constructed; P29-3 follow-up F-h).

Standing sentence: the configuration is a static,
pre-existing throat; no formation mechanism is claimed;
this is not by itself FTL transport.

  (z, z) evolution that produces the P29-3 bulk
  singularity collapses the throat's radial scale D(0, y)
  (2.83 → 0.037 between y = 0 and y*), while the lapse
  A(0, y) stays ≈ 1. The optical cost of crossing z ≈ 0
  is D/A dz, so depth buys crossing cheapness faster
  than the dive (∫dy/A at fixed z ≠ 0) costs. The bulk
  singularity is not incidental to the shortcut — it is
  its engine, and its price: the bulk ends exactly where
  the shortcut would be best.

## 4. Per-bar verdicts

**(i) BULK EXISTENCE — PASS for the √-shape row; FAIL
(not shown) for Option-2.** All gains above are computed
in the P29-3 constructed lift, with paths confined to
y ≤ 0.455 r₀ < y* (checked rows) — inside the bulk that
exists on record. For the Option-2 finite-mass row no
bulk lift exists (P29-3 F-h, explicitly uncomputed):
that row's verdict is capped at PARTIAL — BULK UNSHOWN,
missing bulk = the GN lift of the Option-2 solution.

**(ii) BRANE MATTER LEGALITY — PASS.** The shortcut adds
no brane matter and changes no brane field: the endpoints
sit on the exhibited P27-1/P29-3 dust-minus row (λ = 1),
whose matter is EC-legal on the full domain on record
(ρ ≥ 0 on [r₀, 10³r₀], conserved identically). A null
geodesic is a test ray; nothing is sourced.

**(iii) C12 ACCOUNTING — FAIL for transport (the cap).**
The win exists only under a pre-existing-structure
assumption, stated and labelled here: *the throat, its
legal brane matter, and its bulk slab are assumed to
already exist, spanning both endpoints, before the
decision to signal.* Under that assumption the
decision-inclusive arrival equals the geodesic arrival
(no setup leg is needed — the path is already there).
Without it: formation of the throat is Phase 28's record
— **no classical route exists** — so a construction-
inclusive arrival cannot be stated at any finite price;
priced honestly, the setup leg is not ≥ D/c, it is
*unavailable*. This is stronger than the W2/P22-2
EFFECTIVE ONLY pattern (there the setup cost was finite
and merely losing); here the setup is unpriced because
it is unbuilt and, on record, unbuildable classically.

C12 accounting table (headline pair z_a = 1, at the
P27-1 tidal radius r₀ = 6.802266e6 m, r₀/c = 0.022691 s):

| leg | time |
|---|---|
| brane-confined light, P₋ → P₊ (through throat) | 6.2784 r₀/c = 0.14246 s |
| bulk geodesic arrival, checked (cap y ≤ 0.455) | 3.2618 r₀/c = 0.07401 s |
| saved under pre-existing assumption | 3.0166 r₀/c = 0.06845 s (48.0%) |
| setup / formation leg | **not available on record** (no classical formation route, Phase 28) — decision-inclusive arrival undefined without the labelled assumption |
| carrier conversion (bulk → brane signal at P₊) | no mechanism on record (see bar iv) |

**(iv) CAUSALITY / TOLMAN TEST — NO CTC IN THESE HOMES;
carrier caps transport independently.**
Composition algebra (derived, and verified numerically
in p431_results.json): two channels of effective speed
u > c, the second living in a frame moving at v relative
to the first, return a signal to the sender at
t₂ = L[2u − v(1 + u²/c²)]/(u − v)² (c restored), so
t₂ < 0 — arrival in the sender's own past — iff
**v > v_crit = 2uc²/(u² + c²)**. Checks: t₂ = 0 exactly
at v_crit for u/c = 1.05, 1.5, 3 (v_crit/c = 0.9988,
0.9231, 0.6000), t₂ < 0 above, > 0 below. At this item's
converged headline u_eff = 1.92 c, the counterfactual
threshold is **v_crit = 0.819 c**.
But the composition cannot be run in these homes, for a
structural reason, not a numerical one: there is exactly
**one** static brane and one shortcut, tied to that
brane's rest frame. In that frame every leg has Δt > 0,
and a Lorentz boost of the *description* does not create
a second channel — the antitelephone needs a second,
independently moving braneworld home carrying its own
shortcut, and no such home exists on record (the
exhibited throats are static; a moving-throat braneworld
solution is not in the programme's record). **No CTC
closes at any relative velocity within the existing
homes.** The v_crit number prices only the counterfactual.
Carrier: per W3(f), only bulk fields — gravitons — take
these paths; SM matter and photons are brane-confined in
every construction on record, and no brane↔bulk signal
converter exists on record. Even the real geodesic gain
therefore carries no message-bearing SM signal and no
payload: the transport read is capped by the carrier
alone, independently of C12.

## 5. Verdict (fixed form)

**SHORTCUT REAL, EFFECTIVE ONLY** — for the exhibited
√-shape braneworld home: a bulk null geodesic between
cross-throat brane points arrives in ≈ 0.52× the
brane-confined light time (gain 0.480, checked, at depth
cap y ≤ 0.455 r₀; gain ≈ 0.17 already at cap 0.30 r₀),
computed in a bulk that exists on record, adding no
brane matter. It is EFFECTIVE ONLY, not transport,
because bar (iii) fails (the win is conditional on a
labelled pre-existing-structure assumption whose
formation has no classical route on record) and bar
(iv)'s carrier clause caps it (graviton-only; no SM
signal or payload can take the path; no CTC closes in
these homes at any velocity).
Sub-verdict, Option-2 finite-mass row: **PARTIAL —
BULK UNSHOWN** (missing bulk: its GN lift, P29-3 F-h).
Not SHORTCUT REAL + TRANSPORT → no red-team trigger
under the phase rule (the positive read is a geodesic
read, capped by two bars, and is reported with its
checker reads attached for the coordinator's
verification).

**Reopening conditions.** (1) A formation route for the
throat + bulk (or any priced construction of the pair of
endpoints' connecting structure) would reopen bar (iii)
and the transport read. (2) A construction in which an
SM signal couples to the bulk (a different theory,
gated by W3's D3/D4) would reopen the carrier cap.
(3) For Option-2: constructing its bulk lift (F-h) and
re-running this item's computation in it. (4) A bulk
completion extending past y* (new bulk physics — the
singularity is grid-converged and physical within the
registered GN/Z₂/Λ₅-only construction) would make the
gain's infimum attained and is the only route to an
exact, cap-free gain number.

## 6. Failure protocol (anatomies + follow-ups)

- **F1 — the exact infimum is not attained.** Anatomy:
  the minimiser sits at every imposed depth cap; T(cap)
  decreases monotonically toward y* because D(0, y) → 0
  at the singularity while A stays O(1). This is a
  property of the bulk, not of the optimiser (the cap
  scan is the evidence). Follow-up (mechanism-suggested):
  an asymptotic read of T_inf from the near-singularity
  scaling of D(0, y) — the gain's supremum as a stated
  limit, labelled non-attained. Not run here; the
  capped gains above are the exhibited content.
- **F2 — coarse-resolution overstatement, caught by the
  checker.** The first minimisation (nseg 24, midpoint
  rule) understated T by 5–28% (largest at z_a = 2).
  Anatomy: midpoint-rule quadrature on segments spanning
  the throat's rapid scale variation. Repair: ×10
  subdivision re-evaluation + nseg-48 re-minimisation,
  agreeing to 0.14% on the headline pair; all quoted
  gains are the checked values. Follow-up: nseg-48
  refines for z_a = 0.5, 2 if the coordinator wants
  their second digits.
- **F3 — instrument failures in the first compute run,
  repaired and recorded.** (a) The scalar-evaluated
  minimiser was too slow to finish (numeric gradients
  over per-segment interpolator calls); repaired by
  vectorising the optical functional's evaluation —
  the gate was re-run and re-passed identically after
  the repair (detours ≤ 5.7e-15). (b) The r-chart brane
  integral ∫dr/√(1−r^{−1/2}) has an integrable endpoint
  singularity at r = r₀ that defeated quad at the
  registered tolerance; replaced by the z-chart form
  (integrand finite at the throat), cross-checked
  against an independent trapezoid evaluation to
  ≤ 4.9e-11.
- **F4 — Option-2 has no bulk.** Not a failure of this
  item: the missing bulk is named (its GN lift) and the
  row is capped at PARTIAL — BULK UNSHOWN, per the
  pre-registration. Follow-up = P29-3's F-h, unchanged.
