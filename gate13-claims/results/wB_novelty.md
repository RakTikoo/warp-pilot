## The claim (exact category)

**PAYLOAD TRANSPORT ONLY** — held payload, composed shells.
Bare-shell results are a different category and are NOT the
comparison. Phase 116's composed row e0.10_o0.0725_i0.06: a
nested two-shell warp configuration (inner + outer shell, each
shell's shift derived by integration from an optimised
Procaccia-form source, composed with lapse re-equilibration
holding both shells' orthonormal shifts at their alone values;
outer source-spline span ±0.10W) carrying a held payload of
**0.09758c** at full legality (Type-I 1.000, all
energy-condition margins positive). Predecessor: the same
composition with the outer shell alone at β=0.07, gate-verified
at **0.09556c** (Gate-11, NOVEL). New mechanism element: raising
the composition's payload by raising the OUTER shell's alone β
to **0.0725**, inside the span-narrowed legal window (alone
ceiling bracketed (0.0725, 0.075) at ±0.10W).

## Grade: NOVEL

All three registered questions returned no prior publication:

1. **Composed payload row at/above 0.09758c at full legality —
   none found.** No composed/nested multi-shell warp
   configuration carrying a held payload at or above 0.09758c
   with all energy conditions satisfied (Type-I + margins
   standard or equivalent) appears in the search. The only
   EC-legal warp constructions found are single-shell.
2. **Optimised-source (Procaccia-form) shells as composition
   elements above 0.09556c — none found.** Procaccia's
   optimised-source shell is published only as a single bare
   shell; no publication uses it (or any optimised-source
   shell) as an element of a payload-carrying composition at
   any speed, let alone above 0.09556c.
3. **Raising a composition's payload by raising a constituent
   shell's alone speed inside a span-narrowed legal window —
   none found.** No publication describes a constituent shell's
   alone-β ceiling being moved by span/transition-width
   narrowing and that raised alone β then being spent to raise
   a composition's held payload. The nearest thickness
   neighbour (Le, below) scans thickness in source-first
   classes and finds nothing admissible — a different class
   and the opposite outcome.
