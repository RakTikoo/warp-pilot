**Claim under audit (exact category: PAYLOAD TRANSPORT ONLY — held
payload, composed shells; bare-shell results are a different
category and are NOT the comparison):** Phase 116's composed row
e0.10_o0.0725_i0.06 — a nested two-shell warp configuration (inner +
outer shell, each shell's shift derived by integration from an
optimised Procaccia-form source, composed with lapse
re-equilibration holding both shells' orthonormal shifts at their
alone values; outer source-spline span ±0.10W) carrying a held
payload of 0.09758c at full legality (Type-I 1.000, all
energy-condition margins positive). Predecessor: the same
composition with the outer shell alone at β=0.07, gate-verified at
0.09556c (Gate-11, NOVEL). New mechanism element: raising the
composition's payload by raising the OUTER shell's alone β to
0.0725, inside the span-narrowed legal window (alone ceiling
bracketed (0.0725, 0.075) at ±0.10W).

**Target of the search — the three registered questions:**
(1) any composed/nested multi-shell warp configuration carrying a
held payload at or above 0.09758c at full legality (all energy
conditions, Type-I + margins standard or equivalent);
(2) any published use of optimised-source (Procaccia-form) shells
as elements of a payload-carrying composition above 0.09556c;
(3) any published raising of a composition's payload by raising a
constituent shell's alone speed inside a span/transition-width-
narrowed legal window.

## Result

No composed/nested multi-shell warp configuration carrying a
held payload at or above 0.09758c at full legality was found; no
published use of optimised-source (Procaccia-form) shells as
elements of a payload-carrying composition above 0.09556c was
found; and no published raising of a composition's payload by
raising a constituent shell's alone β inside a span/transition-
width-narrowed legal window was found. The closest neighbours
are all single-shell constructions (Fuchs et al.; Procaccia's
bare shell), a non-legal pocket geometry (Van den Broeck), a
violating payload formalisation (Santiago–Schuster–Visser), or
thickness-as-parameter scans with a negative outcome in a
different construction class (Le arXiv:2605.25417).

## Coverage gaps (stated plainly)

- INSPIRE-HEP and Semantic Scholar were reached only through
  the general web index and arXiv-linked records; no native
  INSPIRE-HEP or Semantic Scholar sessions were run.
- Non-English literature was not searched.
- The borderelliptic.com layered-bubble document (queries 1
  and 5) is a non-peer-reviewed web PDF; it was read at
  abstract/introduction level only.
- The Procaccia continuum-certificate paper is hosted on
  academia.edu (no arXiv ID / DOI was returned for it); it was
  checked at abstract/introduction level via the index copy.
  Its companion optimisation papers were not separately
  fetched; the abstract states the design and the β bracket
  explicitly, and the claim checked — use of the shell in a
  composition — is a property no single-shell certificate
  paper's abstract or introduction asserts.
- No source requiring a live browser (paywalled full texts,
  interactive database sessions) was operated; where a
  publisher page was paywalled (IOP), the arXiv version of the
  same paper was the checked copy.
- Zenodo / general-repository deposits beyond the index hits
  above were not exhaustively swept.
