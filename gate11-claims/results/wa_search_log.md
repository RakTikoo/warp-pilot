**Date of all searches:** 2026-11-23. **Tool:** browser.search /
browser.open (general web index, which indexes arXiv, IOP, SSRN,
academia.edu and publisher pages). **Target of the search:**
nested/composed payload-carrying warp shells at full legality;
source-modulated (Procaccia-form) shell designs used in a
composition; and specifically any published use of source-span /
transition-width narrowing as the binding design variable in a

## Result

No nested/composed payload-carrying warp configuration at full
legality above 0.0799c was found; no use of optimised-source
(Procaccia-form) shells as the shells of such a composition was
found; and no published use of source-span / transition-width
narrowing of a modulated shift source as the binding design
variable was found. The closest thickness-as-parameter neighbour
is Le arXiv:2605.25417 (compactness/thickness scan in source-first
classes, negative outcome) — a different construction class and
the opposite result.
