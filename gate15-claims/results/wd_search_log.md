## Primary-source direct read

### Q23
- Action: `browser.open` on `https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9602053`
  (URL taken verbatim from Q7's returned full-text link family /
  arXiv abstract page). Date: 2026-11-23.
- Result: Schein, F., Aichelburg, P. C. & Israel, W., "String
  Supported Wormhole Spacetimes and Causality Violations",
  submitted 27 Feb 1996. Abstract confirms (verbatim substance):
  static axisymmetric wormhole built from the gravitational field
  of two Schwarzschild particles held apart by strings; wormhole
  obtained by matching two timelike surfaces, one around each
  particle; matching not exact in general, error arbitrarily small
  when mouth separation ≫ mouth radius; unequal mouth masses give
  causality-violating effects. GR. This is the pose-level analogue
  of G15-1 in a different theory home.

## Negative-result summary (what was NOT found, at the searches above)

- No paper, in any index reached, poses two wormhole mouths in a
  common exterior in conformal (Weyl) gravity — exactly, linearly,
  or otherwise.
- No paper applies the BDE conformal junction conditions (or any
  conformal junction set) to a two-centre / non-spherical /
  multipole (ℓ ≥ 1) matching problem.
- No paper computes a wormhole shortcut by evaluating the exterior
  route *inside a constructed two-mouth metric* (its own lapse and
  proper length), in conformal gravity or in any other theory
  reached by these searches. The published transit-time work
  (MMP; Fu–Grado-White–Marolf) is GR/GR+quantum, with an ambient
  exterior.
- These are search results, not proofs of absence. Coverage gaps
  are stated in `wd_novelty.md` §5.
