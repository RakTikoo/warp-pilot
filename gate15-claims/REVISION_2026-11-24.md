## What Phase 230 decided (phase230/results/p2301_memo.md, item (a); checker 13/13 PASS)

- Gate-15's legality content for the two-mouth
  configuration is closure in the full linearised
  Bach system (a pose statement) plus "joining shells
  are legal at both mouths" — and the shell read is
  the single number min Ŝ⁰⁰/α₀ = +0.0202914453
  (Gate-15's fresh-process value +0.0202914452716;
  post-tail +0.0202881661 at ε = 0.1).
- The BDE junction formalism for pure Weyl gravity
  fixes Ŝ⁰⁰ **only**. The shell's tangential surface
  stress Ŝ₂² is not determined by the theory, the
  junction conditions, or anything in the posed
  configuration (programme record: Phase 86 "NOT
  SCORED (BDE fixes Ŝ⁰⁰ only)"; Phase 88 Ŝ₂² only as
  an explicitly labelled UNFITTED datum family;
  Phase 90 the full surface EC set only as a function
  of that family). Gate-15's "legal" therefore asserts
  the WEC-density component of the shell and nothing
  else: NEC_t and DEC_t on the shell were never in
  the accounting. Verdict: **WAS LEGAL ONLY BY
  ACCOUNTING**.
- The deficit, priced: with u := Ŝ₂²/Ŝ⁰⁰,
  NEC_t/α₀ = (1 + u)(0.0202914453),
  DEC_t/α₀ = (1 − |u|)(0.0202914453); u unconstrained
  as posed, so both clauses are unbounded below — an
  unposed component, not a stored negative number.
  On the only tangential datum family on record
  (Phase 88/90, Poisson–Visser barotropic, unfitted)
  the full surface EC set holds exactly for
  u ∈ [−1/2, 1].
- Bulk check clean: the interior read already counts
  the full gravity sector (the Bach tensor is the
  whole gravity operator in conformal gravity); the
  exterior is Bach-flat (B_μν = 0 at the 10⁻²⁵
  evaluation floor, Gate-15 W-A control).
- **Restatement (verbatim):** the two-mouth
  configuration restates as posed closure in the
  linearised Bach system (Gate-15, unchanged) with
  joining shells of legal surface density
  Ŝ⁰⁰/α₀ ≥ +0.0202881661 (post-tail, ε = 0.1) at both
  mouths, whose tangential energy conditions are
  **undetermined by the posed theory** — closable
  only by adjoining a shell matter datum (on record:
  the Phase 88/90 family, window u ∈ [−1/2, 1]).
  "Shells legal" without that qualification does not
  survive the strict audit.
