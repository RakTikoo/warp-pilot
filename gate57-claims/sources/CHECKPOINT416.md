## REGISTRATION DECISION D-416-1 — c idx 13 does NOT widen in Arm A (decided before any search)

**Decision.** Arm A's box widens c idx 0 only. C idx 13
stays at [−2.25, 2.25] in both arms of this phase.

**Argument from the record.**
(i) **Attribution discipline.** The lineage prices one
lever per arm (392: time / widening / modes priced
separately; 408: resume / combination). Where two
coordinates widened together (392 R-W; 408 Arm B), the
charge itself defined both — they were the two
bound-sitters of the subject whose box it was.
FU-P408-2's priced question is c idx 0: A-6's SOLE
bound-sitter, saturated by every subject of the arm
that produced A-6. Folding a second widened coordinate
into the same DE would make any yield unattributable
between the charged lever and the uncharged one.
(ii) **The idx-13 demand evidence is lineage-confounded.**
Every item of it comes from the RW-5-embedding lineage:
the unclipped LS fit wanted −2.31996 at idx 13 (an
overshoot of 0.06996 in an outer mode, Greville
19.230566, support [17.692141, 19.999779]), and 59/64
of Arm B's staged population sits within 0.01 of the
−2.25 bound. But that population descends from a seed
CLIPPED at exactly −2.25 (Amendment A-408-1): DE
populations cluster near their seed's coordinates, so
bound-sitting at the clip site is what inheritance
alone predicts. Powell B-1 remaining on the bound is
only mild independent evidence — a local polish from
the clipped seed. There is no uncontaminated demand
read for idx 13.
(iii) **The independent lineage shows no idx-13
pressure.** A-6 — from the original-box Arm A of 408,
a lineage that never touched the clip — sits at
idx 13 = −2.126915, 0.123 interior; no 408 Arm A
subject was recorded on the idx 13 bound (A-1/A-3/A-5
sat on idx 10 instead). The winning lineage presses
idx 0, not idx 13.
(iv) **A-408-1's spirit.** The amendment rejected
widening idx 13 to accommodate one vector's fit
because it would change the priced question. A
search-box widening is a different act from
seed-fitting — legitimate, but precisely because it
is a separate priced act it belongs in its own
priced question, not folded into idx 0's arm.

**Registered diagnostic (the conversion rule).** Arm
A's harvest records every subject's c idx 13
coordinate. Arm A is seeded from A-6 — a lineage with
no clip at idx 13 — so if Arm A's winners migrate to
the −2.25 bound at idx 13, that IS the uncontaminated
demand read, and it prices a follow-up idx-13 arm
(logged as a follow-up with requirements). If they
stay interior, the Arm B sitting reads as inheritance
and idx 13 leaves the price list. The diagnostic is
reported either way in the anatomy.
