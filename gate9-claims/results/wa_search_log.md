## Full-text reads performed

- **arXiv:1510.05054** (Varieschi & Ault), read in full (abstract,
  §IV, §V, appendix head): their shape/redshift families are
  two-power combinations of (b0/r) (their eq. (16)); ECs are shown
  satisfied **only in the vicinity of the throat** — Φ = 0 example:
  all ECs positive for 1 ≤ r ≲ 1.03, matching to flat/CG vacuum
  required at r ≃ 1.03 with exotic matter at the junction in their
  attempts; Φ ≠ 0 example: ECs satisfied for 1 ≤ r ≲ 1.10; ANEC
  integral **negative** for their Φ = 0 class (−6.35 cm⁻¹ for the
  displayed example), positive for some Φ ≠ 0 cases. No
  whole-domain legality is claimed or shown.
- **arXiv:0801.4401** (Lobo), abstract + §III/IV statements read:
  exact asymptotically flat solutions; a class "satisfies all of the
  energy conditions, in the vicinity of the throat" (their words).
  No legality statement over a domain extending to 10³ r0, and no
  growing power-law shape b = r0(r/r0)^p with p ∈ (0,1), appears.
