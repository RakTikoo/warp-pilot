## Near neighbours found (cited)

- **Cadoni, Modesto, Pitzalis & Sanna, "Stable wormholes in
  conformal gravity", arXiv:2503.14214 [gr-qc] (2025), JCAP
  06 (2025) 016, DOI 10.1088/1475-7516/2025/06/016.** The closest
  neighbour. It proves linear-perturbation stability for a class of
  wormholes built by Weyl rescaling of Minkowski space, analysed in
  detail for a conformally invariant SCALAR-TENSOR theory with a
  Morris–Thorne shape function. Different theory sector (scalar–
  tensor, conformally flat / Weyl-rescaled-Minkowski backgrounds),
  different background (not a matter-held, EC-legal pure-Weyl/Bach
  member with T = 4α_g B joined to a Mannheim–Kazanas exterior),
  different method (no Galerkin quotient spectrum of the linearised
  Bach operator, no static linearised-Bach trace audit). It makes
  the bare headline "stable wormholes in conformal gravity" KNOWN
  as a general statement; it does not contain G9-3's specific
  background, sectors or numbers.
- **Hohmann, Pfeifer, Raidal & Veermäe, "Wormholes in conformal
  gravity", arXiv:1802.02184 [gr-qc] (2018), JCAP 10 (2018) 003,
  DOI 10.1088/1475-7516/2018/10/003.** Wormhole CONSTRUCTION in Weyl
  gravity / conformal scalar–tensor (locally conformal to
  Schwarzschild-(A)dS, vacuum); no perturbation/stability analysis.
- **Lobo, "General class of wormhole geometries in conformal Weyl
  gravity", arXiv:0801.4401 [gr-qc] (2008).** Geometries + energy
  conditions in conformal Weyl gravity; no linearised-Bach
  stability / spectrum computation.
- **Bronnikov & Grinyok, "Conformal continuations and wormhole
  instability in scalar-tensor gravity", arXiv:gr-qc/0411063
  (2004), Grav. Cosmol. 10 (2004) 237.** Spherical perturbations of
  scalar–tensor (conformal-continuation) wormholes — all UNSTABLE.
  Different theory (scalar–tensor, Einstein frame Fisher solution),
  opposite verdict; not a Bach-operator analysis.
- Vacuum static wormhole in conformal gravity, arXiv:0907.1128
  (2009): solution only, no stability spectrum.
- Generic wormhole radial-perturbation literature in OTHER
  theories (Ellis–Bronnikov phantom-scalar GR: Cremona, Pizzocchero
  & Sarbach, arXiv:1911.13103; charged EB, arXiv:2507.02258;
  Harada/conformal-Killing thin-shell wormholes, arXiv:2407.08945):
  methods and instability results for second-order theories; none
  treats the fourth-order Bach operator on a Morris–Thorne
  background, and none uses a Galerkin quotient spectrum of the
  quadratic Weyl action.
- Fourth-order-gravity perturbation machinery on NON-wormhole
  backgrounds exists (e.g. Mauro et al., Eur. Phys. J. Plus 130
  (2015) 135, DOI 10.1140/epjp/i2015-15135-0, Schwarzschild
  stability in auxiliary-field form) — no wormhole application
  found.
