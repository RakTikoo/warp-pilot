## §6 — Incidents and disclosures

1. **CAL-7 first pass (weak reference).** The worst-null solver's
   first calibration compared it against a 642-direction scan with a
   single-start polish and showed a spurious 0.0444 deviation — the
   reference had missed a narrow basin. A stronger reference
   (fib(5000) + polish) and the solver's KKT certificate (residual
   7.7×10⁻¹⁵) vindicated the solver; the 300-tensor stress test found
   zero violations. The deciding reads used the certified solver.
2. **CAL-4 finite-difference second derivatives.** At h = 10⁻⁴ the FD
   check on ∂∂g disagrees with the analytic operator by 2.2×10⁻⁵
   (random points) / 1.3×10⁻⁵ (knot-free points), while ∂g agrees at
   1.9×10⁻⁹. The h-sweep (CAL-4c) shows FD converging to the analytic
   values as h grows to 4×10⁻⁴ (agreement 2.1×10⁻⁷) and diverging
   ∝ eps/h² for smaller h — the FD method's roundoff floor with
   intermediate |g| ~ 80, not operator error. The operator layer is
   certified independently by CAL-5, CAL-6, CAL-2 and the controls.
3. **Sampled-fallback frame convention at non-Type-I points.** Where
   a SET minimum is attained at a non-Type-I point, the stored and
   gate values differ: CTL-C NEC minimum −1.02×10⁻⁸ and μ_S = 0.5
   joint +6.9×10⁻⁹; CTL-B NEC span 6.8×10⁻⁷ across three
   implementations; C395 NEC −3.7×10⁻⁷, WEC −3.4×10⁻⁶; and the
   winner's fib-scan worst-null −4.0×10⁻⁹ (a discrete scan in frame
   coordinates; the exact secular value agrees at −1.4×10⁻¹⁷). The
   mechanism is the C-10 one: the sampled margins are contractions
   over a fixed direction set in the Eulerian frame, whose triad
   basis inside γ's degenerate tangential plane is LAPACK-arbitrary
   and rotates under 1×10⁻¹⁷-level differences in γ. Frame-invariant
   companions (Type-I fractions, argmin radii, Type-I-only minima,
   and every minimum whose argmin is Type-I) agree at roundoff in
   every case. No deciding value in this gate sits at a non-Type-I
